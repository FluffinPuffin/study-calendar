📅 StudyCalendar

Plan smarter.
Study better.

StudyCalendar helps you schedule and track your study sessions throughout the week. Build your routine, stay on top of your subjects, and see your progress — all in the browser, no account needed.

See the Features

What you can do

  • 📅

    Weekly Calendar View

    See all your study sessions laid out by day so you can spot gaps and plan ahead.

  • ✎

    Add, Edit & Delete Sessions

    Create study blocks with a subject, start time, duration, and optional notes.

  • 🎨

    Subject Color Coding

    Assign a color to each subject so you can tell them apart at a glance.

  • ✅

    Session Completion Tracking

    Check off finished sessions and watch your weekly completion percentage climb.

  • 💾

    Auto-Save with localStorage

    Your schedule saves automatically in the browser — no login, no backend required.
